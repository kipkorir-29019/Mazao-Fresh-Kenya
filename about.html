About Mazao Fresh Kenya

Mazao Fresh Kenya is an agricultural business dedicated to providing fresh, quality and affordable farm produce. We aim to connect farmers with consumers while promoting local agriculture and healthy living across Kenya.

Our Mission

Our mission is to connect smallholder farmers growing produce to buyers in urban markets including Nairobi, Nakuru and Eldoret.

Our Objectives

  • Provide fresh, safe and quality farm produce.
  • Support local farmers by creating market opportunities.
  • Offer affordable prices to our customers.
  • Promote healthy eating and nutrition.
  • Improve access to agricultural products.
